NON-LENDING REQUESTS

Order an Appraisal

Information for homeowners, attorneys, trustees, personal representatives, and other potential private clients.

Current practice note: Mortgage/lending appraisal work is the primary practice area. Non-lending assignments are not currently the primary focus and are accepted only when the assignment is appropriate and can be performed within the appraiser's competency and scope.

How a private request will work

  1. Tell us about the assignment. Provide the property address, intended use, effective date if applicable, and a brief description of what you need.
  2. We determine whether the assignment is appropriate. Not every request can or should be accepted.
  3. Receive the engagement and fee information. Payment instructions can be provided for accepted non-lending assignments.
  4. Schedule the inspection and complete the assignment. Timing depends on property access, assignment scope, market research, and reporting requirements.

Payment

For accepted non-lending assignments, electronic payment options such as PayPal or Zelle can be made available. Payment identifiers and instructions will be added here once the service is active.
